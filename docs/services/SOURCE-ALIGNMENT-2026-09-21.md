## Safe page content grounded in current GitHub

- Lokahi Connect offers individualized online student learning for all ages; literacy instruction, educational therapy, ADHD coaching, or academic support may be included based on fit. Center families in examples without implying adult learners are excluded.
- 30-minute online session: $75. 50-minute online session: $120. Optional comprehensive profile and written service plan, when appropriate: $425. Do not relabel this as mandatory diagnostic assessment or universally required intake.
- Availability is confirmed individually. An inquiry is not enrollment, placement, or a confirmed appointment.
- Limited financial assistance may be available depending on funding and capacity. Do not imply a scholarship is guaranteed.
- Stephanie Steinshouer is Founder and Executive Director and an educational therapist. The website states CERI and NILD certifications and WABIDA board service; a credential is not evidence that the offered program follows formal NILD programming.
- Lokahi Connect is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 33-3985311, with roots in Bainbridge Island and Maui. This is not a statewide in-person availability claim.
- Use public-facing language about learner agency, meaningful context, language relationships, investigation, evidence, and guided interaction. An example: “Together, we investigate how spelling, pronunciation, word structure, and context work together. Learners have room to notice, ask questions, test ideas, and explain their thinking.”
- “Research-informed” and “grounded in morphological research” are supported bounded descriptions. Full Lokahi-model efficacy is not established by these materials.
- EF framing should name what is practiced or made accessible: visible steps, planning, noticing when a strategy helps, revising an approach, reflection. No promises to repair executive function, increase working-memory capacity, rewire a brain, or cure ADHD/dyslexia.
- Emotional copy may invite safety and agency: “Tell us what learning has been like and what you hope could feel different.” Do not infer a family's emotions from behavior or present confidence, reduced conflict, or improved regulation as assured outcomes.

## Conflicts and limits to carry into implementation

1. Canonical mission and current website say flexible recursive inquiry, no automatically primary form. README/FOUNDATIONS retain meaning-first hierarchy and fixed sequence; central-claim-and-scope is marked internal draft and includes overbroad oral-language assumptions. Do not propagate those into the page. Preserve existing unrelated files.
2. AGENTS and older grant boilerplate expand WORD differently. Canonical approved source says do not expand WORD without official approval. Use “Lokahi WORD Project” alone.
3. Current website says CERI/NILD broadly but does not support the exact UC Riverside certification wording or precise C-SLDI expansion. The user-provided context supports those; identify that provenance if included, or use simply “educational therapist” and link to About. Do not advertise formal NILD programming; the user's explicit clarification excludes it.
4. Current GitHub pages reviewed do not confirm Google Meet or a free Friday 30-minute consultation; these are user-context facts, not verified repository facts. If page includes them, trace to user context and avoid newly promising availability.
5. Outcomes menu is a goals/metrics menu and includes dated fixed sequence. No measured results, testimonials, completion rates, or effect sizes can be inferred from it.
6. Existing Programs inquiry only prepares an email in a user's device and expressly does not send/save data. New contact flow must distinguish actual submission from email preparation and claim receipt only on verified success. It should collect adult contact and broad goals, not child names, evaluation uploads, diagnosis detail, school/IEP/504 or health records. New service notification recipient must be Stephanie as requested, not existing info@ default.
