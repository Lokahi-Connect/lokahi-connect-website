# Remote literacy consultation campaign

Organic pilot for the Lokahi Connect organization accounts. Stephanie approves release and replies to inquiries. Allow one week between posts on each channel.

## Week 1 · Facebook · Try a word investigation

“We play the song.” “We replay the song.” Which sentence tells you someone has already heard it? What did adding **re** change?

Try the question together. Let each person point to the part of the word that helped them decide. If you’d like to explore how this kind of investigation could fit your learner, bring a reading or writing question to a free 30-minute online consultation with Lokahi Connect.

https://www.lokahiconnect.org/services.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=remote_literacy&utm_content=replay

## Week 1 · LinkedIn · Educator referrals

When a family asks what online literacy support looks like, it helps to have an example. Lokahi Connect’s free guide follows a learner investigating a short written request and deciding how to reply. Share it with a family weighing remote support. They can also request a free conversation about their goals and session availability.

https://www.lokahiconnect.org/online-structured-literacy.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=remote_literacy&utm_content=guide

## Week 2 · Facebook · Family questions

What would you want an online literacy instructor to know about how your learner participates? Maybe they like moving word parts, pointing to a choice, or having time to think before answering. Bring one example to a free 30-minute conversation with Lokahi Connect. We can talk about reading and writing goals and whether individual remote sessions might fit.

https://www.lokahiconnect.org/services.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=remote_literacy&utm_content=participation

## Week 2 · LinkedIn · Adult learners

“Can you move the meeting?” A reader might picture a different room or a different time. What would you ask before replying? Everyday messages give adult learners a place to explore how context shapes meaning. Adults interested in individual remote reading or writing support can ask Lokahi Connect about fit and availability during a free online consultation.

https://www.lokahiconnect.org/services.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=remote_literacy&utm_content=context
