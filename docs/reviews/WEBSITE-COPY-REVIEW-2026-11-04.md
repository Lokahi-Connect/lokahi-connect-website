Reviewed November 4, 2026 (Hawaiʻi time).
Owner: Stephanie Steinshouer. Status: approved for publication by Stephanie on November 4, 2026 (Hawaiʻi time).

## Outcome

Reviewed the website's 18 HTML pages. The main issues were dense language, a narrow parent-facing voice, unexplained examples, and repeated cautionary wording. The draft welcomes children, teens, adults, caregivers, educators, and community partners while keeping learning goals and relationships central.

## Corrections and clarifications

- Programs displayed an August 20 update label alongside an October 31 service update. Replaced the stale top label with “Explore current options”; retained the actual service-review date.
- Programs' role selector did not offer adults seeking their own support. Added that option to the existing email-preparation form.
- Inquiry instructions asked adults for their names while telling visitors not to include learner names. Clarified the distinction between the adult's contact details and other people's identifying information or private records.
- The Our Approach page contained internal editorial directions (“Use language such as…”). Replaced these with a direct explanation of what informs the practice and the limits of evidence.
- Replaced the unexplained sign/signature/designate word sums with a short play/replay/playful example, an explanation of notation, sentence context, and questions about what stays connected and what changes. This is a clarity revision; no unsupported verdict about the old historical analyses is asserted.
- The Dyslexia page's “Begin with…” instruction could imply a required starting point. Changed it to “Explore…” and preserved flexible movement among the Four Questions.
- Changed “Log a resource interaction” into a reader-facing feedback invitation. The destination and tracking behavior are unchanged.
