# Lokahi Connect Website — External Source and Link Review

- Review date: 2026-08-20
- Scope: public claims, source descriptions, outside links, embedded services, and geography labels in the website update
- Disposition: verified for publication with the limits recorded below

## Corrections made

1. Replaced the homepage’s obsolete 2022 NAEP statistic with the 2024 Grade 4 result and retained NAEP’s warning that “Proficient” is not the same as grade-level proficiency.
2. Removed the 2020 Gallup hypothetical economic estimate and replaced it with the 2023 NCES PIAAC national literacy result, with a population-level interpretation boundary.
3. Corrected the Student Lab call to action. It now opens the Lokahi WORD Project Student Lab rather than the separate English orthography presentation.
4. Removed WordWorks Kingston from the trusted-source list because its ordered presentation of the Four Questions conflicts with Lokahi Connect’s approved flexible, recursive implementation language.
5. Added a creator-authored SWI overview, an independent randomized trial, and a narrower description of Bowers and Kirby (2010). The website does not present SWI as a complete dyslexia intervention or a replacement for comprehensive literacy instruction.
6. Added TutorBird’s privacy policy next to the embedded Parent Portal.
7. Separated Hawaiʻi statewide resources from Maui-specific resources and retained eligibility limits where a resource is not open to every visitor.

## Interpretation and access limits

- A working link does not establish endorsement, current capacity, eligibility, quality, or a referral relationship.
- Dyslexia and school-navigation pages provide general information, not diagnosis, legal advice, or a determination of eligibility.
- Some International Dyslexia Association pages block automated requests. Their public page destinations and content were reviewed in a browser; an automated HTTP 403 is not treated as a broken public link.
- The Givebutter destination identifies Lokahi Connect and EIN 33-3985311, but its public campaign title and some campaign language still use earlier “Meaning-First Literacy” wording. The campaign should receive a separate owner-side content alignment before it is treated as fully brand-aligned. This website update does not change the external campaign.
- The Airtable destination is a resource-interaction log, not the family and community questionnaire. The two pathways remain distinct.

## Ongoing maintenance

External links and descriptions should be reviewed periodically because agencies, nonprofit organizations, publications, eligibility rules, and URLs can change.
