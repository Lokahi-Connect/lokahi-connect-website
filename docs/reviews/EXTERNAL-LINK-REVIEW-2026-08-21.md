# Family & Community Toolkit — External Source and Link Review

- Review date: 2026-08-21
- Scope: public guidance, state-process descriptions, crisis routing, and outside links added for the Family & Community Toolkit
- Disposition: verified for bounded publication with the limits recorded below

## Sources reviewed

### Federal evaluation guidance

- International Dyslexia Association, Testing and Evaluation
  - Destination: `https://dyslexiaida.org/testing-and-evaluation/`
  - Supports: screening identifies learners at risk; dyslexia diagnosis requires a broader evaluation using history, observations, testing, and professional interpretation rather than one screener or score.
- U.S. Department of Education, IDEA Part B evaluation regulations
  - Destination: `https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/d`
  - Supports: screening for instructional purposes is distinct from evaluation; evaluations and eligibility determinations are separate processes.
- U.S. Department of Education, Child Find guidance
  - Destination: `https://sites.ed.gov/idea/idea-files/rts-qa-child-find-part-b-08-24-2021/`
  - Supports: MTSS or intervention is not a required prerequisite to evaluation and must not be used to delay or deny evaluation when a disability is suspected.
- U.S. Department of Education, Disability Discrimination FAQs
  - Destination: `https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/civil-rights-laws/disability-discrimination/frequently-asked-questions-disability-discrimination`
  - Supports: a medical diagnosis is not a precondition to a public-school Section 504 evaluation or determination.
- U.S. Department of Education, Section 504 FAPE FAQs
  - Destination: `https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/civil-rights-laws/disability-discrimination/frequently-asked-questions-section-504-free-appropriate-public-education-fape`
  - Supports: a medical diagnosis may be considered with other evidence but does not automatically establish Section 504 services or eligibility.

## Publication limits

- The toolkit provides general educational navigation, not diagnosis, individualized legal advice, an eligibility determination, provider endorsement, crisis response, or a guarantee of service.
- Washington and Hawaiʻi procedures are presented separately. One state’s process or timeline must not be generalized to the other.
- Timelines and agency procedures can change and should be periodically rechecked against the responsible state source.
- A working link does not prove current capacity, eligibility, or quality.
- A diagnosis, screening result, private report, or school meeting does not automatically establish IDEA or Section 504 eligibility.
- The workplace section routes visitors to official EEOC guidance and does not determine whether an individual, employer, or requested adjustment is covered.
- The 988 reference is a crisis-routing boundary. Lokahi Connect does not monitor the website for emergencies and does not provide crisis response.
- The toolkit requests no learner records or identifying information and directs visitors to use an agreed secure channel before sharing protected records.

## Ongoing maintenance

Recheck state-process descriptions, timelines, postsecondary guidance, workplace guidance, and crisis-routing information whenever an agency page changes and at regular content-maintenance intervals.
