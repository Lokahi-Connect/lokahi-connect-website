## 1. Launch post
Every learner has ideas worth sharing—a story to tell, a question to explore, a message to put into words.

Lokahi Connect’s Be the Connection fundraiser helps make individualized reading, spelling, writing and comprehension support more affordable for children, teens and adults.

Our first goal is $9,600: the tuition value of four weeks of support for ten learners. Gifts are pooled for full or partial tuition grants and service delivery. Awards depend on funding, educational fit and capacity.

Give once, give monthly, or share the appeal: https://givebutter.com/fund-a-students-tuition-biuo57

## 2. Short sharing post
A gift does not have to fund a whole tuition grant to help. Be the Connection brings gifts together to make individualized literacy support more affordable. Explore the appeal, choose an amount that works for you, or share it with someone who cares about access to learning.
