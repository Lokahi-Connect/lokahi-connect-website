Your Gift in Action

Every giving level translates directly into student outcomes, educator capacity, and open tools for families.

Most ImpactfulRoot$10,000

Who Can Help

There's a Role for You

Donors, educators, researchers, school leaders, and families all play a part in building a more literate world.

🎓

Educators

Bring SWI-based professional development to your school. We offer single-day institutes, multi-day cohorts, and year-long coaching to build lasting instructional capacity.

🔬

Researchers

Collaborate with us on morphological intervention studies. We're building an evidence base for SWI with neurodivergent populations and welcome research partnerships.

🏫

Schools & Districts

Partner with us for student services, curriculum alignment, or educator certification. We work flexibly with schools to meet your students' needs and your professional development goals.

👨‍👩‍👧

Families

Refer a student, access the free Student Lab, or connect with our community of parents who advocate for meaning-based literacy. No family should navigate this alone.
