About Lokahi Connect

We are a literacy nonprofit with roots in Maui, Hawaiʻi, and Bainbridge Island, Washington. Our work brings learners, families, and educators into the investigation of written language.

Why the project began

Lokahi Connect began with a fundraising and teaching goal. We wanted more people to have access to Structured Word Inquiry and a place to share what they discovered. The Lokahi WORD Project grew out of that purpose.

Today we develop word investigations, share free materials, and invite people who use them to help shape the next resource.

Our founder

Stephanie Steinshouer

Founder and Executive Director

Stephanie is an educational therapist. Her work on the Lokahi WORD Project starts with the questions learners bring to a word, a sentence, or a story.

She serves on the Board of Directors for the Washington State Branch of the International Dyslexia Association. View the WABIDA board.

Professional background

Stephanie holds an educational therapy certificate from the University of California, Riverside, the CERI Certified Structured Literacy Dyslexia Interventionist (C-SLDI) credential, and an IDA Structured Literacy Educator (SLE) certificate.
