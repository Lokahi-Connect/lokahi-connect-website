How the fund works

A clear purpose for every tuition gift.

What if the goal is not fully met—or gifts exceed it?

Available funds can support fewer full grants or partial tuition reductions. Gifts beyond the first goal can support additional tuition assistance and future four-week awards for the same purpose. Lokahi Connect confirms funding, educational fit, and scheduling capacity before offering an award.

How do monthly gifts relate to four-week grants?

One-time and recurring gifts both support the pooled tuition fund. Monthly donations are billed by calendar month; each grant covers four weeks. A recurring gift does not automatically renew a learner’s grant. Renewal depends on continued funding and capacity.

Does this appeal also fund professional development?

No. Tuition gifts are dedicated to full or partial tuition assistance and delivering those services. Professional learning Lokahi offers to educators and continuing training for Lokahi’s practitioners are supported separately through general giving and other funding.

How can a learner or family ask about assistance?

Use the service inquiry to request a free consultation and ask about current tuition-assistance availability. The fund’s goal does not mean that 10 awards are already available. Services provide educational instruction and planning, not a clinical diagnosis.
