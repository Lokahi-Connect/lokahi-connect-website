Online reading, dyslexia, and educational therapy support.

You see your learner’s ideas and interests. You may also see the effort that reading, spelling, writing, comprehension, or getting started can take. Lokahi Connect offers individualized online instruction for Maui families, families on Bainbridge Island, and learners elsewhere who can participate remotely. Sessions use a comprehensive, evidence-based Structured Literacy approach to reading and writing instruction, as explained by IDA.

Start with what matters to you

What would you like support with?

Tell us what your learner enjoys, where support would help, and what you hope will become easier. Services are shaped around individual goals and fit.

Reading words takes effort

Investigate relationships among spelling, pronunciation, word structure, and meaning. Explicit instruction and supported reading are adapted to the learner’s questions, including dyslexia-related reading needs.

Reading the words, but missing the meaning

Explore vocabulary, phrases, sentences, and connected text. Explain an interpretation, return to the text for evidence, and consider how wording and context shape meaning.

Spelling does not seem to stick

Use word sums, related words, and spelling evidence to investigate how words are built. Connect pronunciation and written structure with the meaning the learner wants to express.

Ideas are easier to say than to write

Use conversation, oral rehearsal, visible planning, and sentence work to develop ideas. Read and revise writing together so the intended message becomes clearer.

Starting and organizing work feels difficult

Make the task and its steps visible, choose a manageable starting point, and reflect on what helped. Educational therapy, executive-function support, or ADHD coaching may be included based on fit.

We need help choosing the next step

Discuss your observations, the learner’s strengths, and priorities. A written learning profile and service plan may help guide instruction; the optional plan is not a diagnostic evaluation.

How we teach

Comprehensive, individualized literacy instruction.

Lokahi Connect offers online reading and writing instruction grounded in Structured Literacy—an evidence-based, integrated approach that develops oral language and explicitly teaches written language. Learn more about how we work or read IDA’s definition of Structured Literacy.

Practical questions

A clearer picture before you begin.

What happens in the free 30-minute consultation?

You meet online with Stephanie to discuss Lokahi Connect’s approach, share your goals and questions, and explore whether the available support is a fit. This initial conversation is free, with no obligation to enroll. Stephanie confirms the appointment by email. Student instruction and any optional learning profile are arranged separately.

Do you work with families on Maui?

Yes. Lokahi Connect welcomes inquiries from Maui and elsewhere in Hawaiʻi, as well as Bainbridge Island, Washington, and other locations. Current student sessions are live and online. Tell Stephanie your time zone so you can discuss a workable schedule.

Is this tutoring or educational therapy?

Families may arrive looking for a reading tutor or dyslexia support. Stephanie’s individualized educational therapy connects literacy instruction with the learning strategies and supports needed to participate, explain ideas, and use learning in new situations. The initial conversation helps define the scope for your learner.

Does this include speech-language therapy or a diagnosis?

These services focus on educational instruction in reading, spelling, writing, comprehension, and learning strategies. They do not include speech-language pathology, occupational therapy, or a clinical diagnosis. If that is the support you are seeking, discuss the appropriate professional with Stephanie.

What will we need for online sessions?

A reliable internet connection, a computer with a camera and microphone, and a place where the learner can participate comfortably. Stephanie will confirm the meeting link, device setup, and any shared materials before sessions begin. Tell her about access needs so you can discuss them together.

What if online learning has felt difficult before?

Tell Stephanie what helped and what made participation difficult. You can discuss pacing, session length, visible steps, and ways to respond. Whether the remote format fits the learner is part of the initial conversation.

Is this only for children?

No. Current online student-learning services are open to inquiries for learners of all ages. Adults seeking support for themselves are welcome to contact Stephanie.

Can we discuss executive-function support?

Yes. Share the everyday task you would like help with—for example, starting a project, organizing its steps, or checking whether a strategy is useful. Stephanie can discuss whether the available support fits your goals.

Will we need the $425 profile and plan?

It is optional and offered when appropriate. The written learning profile and service plan help guide educational instruction; this is not a diagnostic evaluation. Before purchasing, agree with Stephanie on the assessment or learning activities, number and length of meetings, what the written profile and plan will cover, and whether a feedback meeting is included. Any work outside the agreed scope is discussed separately before it begins.

How can I stay involved?

Share what you and your learner notice, what feels useful, and where questions remain. During planning, discuss how you would like to communicate and review goals. Current families can also access the Parent Portal for session notes, scheduling, and progress updates.
