A person to think alongside

An active conversation about learning.

Stephanie guides learners to notice relationships, explain their thinking, and test possibilities. Within the Lokahi WORD Project approach, language is explored through connected inquiry. A response offers something to investigate, rather than a complete judgment of what a learner understands.

Clear steps, visible information, and flexible ways to respond support participation. The learner’s observations and your family’s perspective help inform the work.

A glimpse of the approach

Notice. Investigate. Reflect.

A meaningful sentence can open a discussion. The learner notices something about a word or an idea. Together, learner and practitioner compare evidence, try an explanation, and return to the sentence to see what makes sense.

Reflection might begin with: “What helped you decide? Where else could you try that?”

Practical questions

A clearer picture before you begin.

What will we need for online sessions?

A reliable internet connection, a computer with a camera and microphone, and a place where the learner can participate comfortably. Stephanie will confirm the meeting link, device setup, and any shared materials before sessions begin. Tell her about access needs so you can discuss them together.

What if online learning has felt difficult before?

Tell Stephanie what helped and what made participation difficult. You can discuss pacing, session length, visible steps, and ways to respond. Whether the remote format fits the learner is part of the initial conversation.

Is this only for children?

No. Current online student-learning services are open to inquiries for learners of all ages. Adults seeking support for themselves are welcome to contact Stephanie.

Can we discuss executive-function support?

Yes. Share the everyday task you would like help with—for example, starting a project, organizing its steps, or checking whether a strategy is useful. Stephanie can discuss whether the available support fits your goals.

Will we need the $425 profile and plan?

It is optional and offered when appropriate. Ask Stephanie whether it would be useful and what it would include for your situation. It is not presented here as a diagnostic evaluation.

How can I stay involved?

Share what you and your learner notice, what feels useful, and where questions remain. During planning, discuss how you would like to communicate and review goals. Current families can also access the Parent Portal for session notes, scheduling, and progress updates.
