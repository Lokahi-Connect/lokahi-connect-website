Your choice matters

Website Privacy & Analytics

Understand what this website measures, what it does not collect, and how to change your analytics choice.

Updated: November 4, 2026Google Measurement ID: G-727LCMWN8K

Analytics is optional

Lokahi Connect uses Google Analytics only after a visitor chooses Allow analytics. Before that choice, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded and no analytics data is sent to Google from this website.

If analytics is allowed, Lokahi Connect uses aggregated website information to understand which pages and resources are useful, identify navigation problems, and improve access to information.

Review your choice at any time. Selecting the button below opens the same clear allow-or-decline choice shown on a first visit.

Remote service inquiries

The Services page links to an inquiry form hosted by Airtable. Airtable receives and stores your response for Lokahi Connect. The form collects your adult contact name, email, contact consent, and any optional phone number, support interest, general message, and answer to “How did you hear about us?” that you provide. Submission time and the form source are recorded automatically. You do not need an Airtable account to submit an inquiry.

Inquiry notifications are emailed to stephanie@lokahiconnect.org. Stephanie uses this information to respond about services and next steps. Inquiry records include internal follow-up status, enrollment progress, and brief notes. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to marketing, confirm an appointment, or enroll you in services. The form’s confirmation records submission; it does not prove that an email notification has reached an inbox.

Review Airtable’s Privacy Policy ↗ before submitting. If you use the direct email alternative, your email provider and Lokahi Connect’s email service handle that message.

Please provide your adult contact details and a general question only. Do not include other people’s names, diagnoses, evaluations, school records, or other sensitive information. Ask Stephanie about an appropriate intake process if records are needed. Lokahi Connect does not intentionally send inquiry contents to its website analytics. To ask about your inquiry or request removal of its contact information, email stephanie@lokahiconnect.org.

What may be measured

When a visitor allows analytics, Google Analytics may receive information ordinarily associated with website measurement, including:

  • the page viewed, page title, and referring website;
  • general browser, device, and screen information;
  • approximate geographic information derived by Google;
  • visit timing and interaction information; and
  • bounded events such as selecting the community resource-feedback link, opening a service inquiry form or email link, or arriving at the inquiry confirmation page after an Airtable submission redirect.

Service inquiry click measurement records only the contact route (form or email) and the website page. If a visitor allows analytics, opens the Airtable form in the same browser tab, submits it, and Airtable redirects back to the website confirmation page, the site may record a separate service_inquiry_submitted event. It includes only the contact method and confirmation page path; it does not include the form response, referral answer, record ID, or enrollment status. Visitors who decline analytics or do not return through the confirmation redirect are not counted as submissions in Google Analytics. Form responses, referral answers, and enrollment progress stay in Lokahi Connect’s inquiry records; they are not sent to Google Analytics.

Google advertising and cross-device signals remain off. Lokahi Connect disables Google advertising storage, advertising personalization, advertising user data, and Google Signals in the website configuration. Google Analytics is used to improve the website, not to build advertising audiences. OpenAI ad measurement has its own optional choice, described below.

What is not intentionally sent to Google Analytics

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses;
  • student, school, evaluation, disability, health, IEP, or 504 records;
  • the contents of questions or email messages prepared through this website;
  • payment-card, bank-account, or donation-transaction information; or
  • credentials entered into the Tutorbird Parent Portal.

Please do not enter private learner records into ordinary website, email, feedback, or donation communications.

Optional OpenAI ad measurement

Ad measurement is off until you choose Allow ad measurement. Your Google Analytics choice does not enable it. Declining ad measurement does not affect access to services or the inquiry form.

With your permission, the Services and inquiry-confirmation pages load the OpenAI Pixel to measure page visits and an inquiry after Airtable returns you to the confirmation page. The Pixel sends event information, the website origin, browser and connection information, and available advertising identifiers to OpenAI. It may store first-party cookies: an ad click reference for up to 30 days and a browser reference for up to 365 days. Browser restrictions may shorten these periods.

Lokahi Connect does not provide names, contact details, form answers, learner records, diagnoses, or enrollment information to the Pixel. It is not installed inside Airtable, the Parent Portal, donation forms, or the website’s other forms. Events opt out of future user-level personalization. Review OpenAI’s Privacy Policy.

To avoid counting a form opening as an inquiry, browser submission measurement requires a recent form opening in the same tab and a return from Airtable. A random event identifier and start time are kept in that tab’s session storage. The identifier is consumed once when the event is queued. Missing consent, blocked storage, a blocked Pixel, a hidden referrer, a different tab, or a return after 30 minutes can prevent measurement. Airtable inquiry records remain the source of truth; ad reporting is not a complete count of inquiries.

The website remembers this separate choice in local storage. Choosing Keep ad measurement off stops future events and clears the known first-party measurement cookies. It does not delete information already received by OpenAI. No server-side conversion integration is enabled by this website.

May we measure whether our ads are helpful?

If you allow it, OpenAI can measure visits and an inquiry submitted through this website, using browser information and ad identifiers. Your form answers stay in Airtable. This choice is separate from Google Analytics and is not required to contact us.

Ad measurement is off. You can still send an inquiry.

Ad measurement privacy details

How your choice works

The website stores the words granted or denied in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your analytics choice. If you clear browser storage or use another browser or device, the website will ask again.

When analytics is allowed, Google may use first-party analytics cookies to distinguish visits and sessions. Review Google’s Privacy Policy ↗ for information about Google’s handling of data.

Changing a choice to “No thanks” stops future analytics collection from this browser. It does not delete information previously collected while analytics was allowed.

External services have separate practices

The Give page contains an embedded Givebutter donation form. Opening that page loads content from Givebutter and its service providers so the form can display and protect transactions. Givebutter may receive technical information ordinarily associated with loading an embedded service, such as an IP address, browser or device information, the referring page, and campaign-attribution information.

Information entered into the donation form is submitted directly to Givebutter and its payment providers. Lokahi Connect does not intentionally send payment-card, bank-account, or donation-form information entered inside the Givebutter form to Google Analytics. Review Givebutter’s Privacy Policy ↗ and Terms of Use ↗ before donating.

Links to other external resources, email services, and social platforms leave the Lokahi Connect website and are governed by those providers’ privacy practices.

The Tutorbird Parent Portal is an embedded service. Lokahi Connect’s analytics cannot read credentials or private information entered inside the Tutorbird-controlled portal frame.

Questions or access concerns

For questions about this notice or help changing your website choice, email info@lokahiconnect.org. Do not include learner records or other sensitive information in an ordinary email.
