How an investigation takes shape

A learner might ask why replay and playful share a spelling. A project example puts each word in a sentence, shows how its parts fit, and invites readers to test what changes in meaning. We use those questions when developing materials for learners and educators.

Read the worked example →Read the research →

What we’re developing

Our work includes writing word-family examples, making investigations easier to navigate, and preparing guidance for educators. Feedback about a confusing example or a missing resource helps us decide what to revise.
