Reading, spelling, and writing support for adults.

Lokahi Connect offers individualized online literacy instruction and educational therapy for adults, including adults with dyslexia. Work toward your own reading, spelling, comprehension, and writing goals with Stephanie Steinshouer.

Your goals and experience belong at the center. Begin with a free 30-minute online consultation to discuss your questions and whether this support fits.

Start with what you want to do

Support connected to your life.

A goal can come from everyday life, work, study, or an interest you want to pursue. These are possible starting points, not a checklist for identifying dyslexia.

Read and spell words

Investigate relationships among spelling, pronunciation, word structure, and meaning, with explicit teaching and supported practice suited to your current knowledge.

Understand what you read

Work with vocabulary, sentence relationships, and connected text. Compare interpretations and look for evidence in the wording and context.

Develop and communicate ideas

Plan, draft, and revise writing for a purpose that matters to you. Conversation, typing, visible planning, or other agreed response supports can help make your thinking available.

Difficulty with a task does not establish its cause. Your questions, strengths, language experience, prior instruction, and the supports available all matter when planning educational work.

Your participation matters

A plan made with you.

Instruction draws on Structured Literacy: explicit, systematic teaching of oral and written language, with practice and review adjusted to the learner. Within the Lokahi WORD Project, language inquiry connects spelling, morphology, pronunciation, grammar, meaning, and context. No single activity or fixed order defines every session.

You can discuss what has and has not helped before, choose meaningful goals, and ask for a different way to participate. Online fit includes pacing, session length, visual access, ways to respond, and the demands of using the technology.

Progress review considers your work, your explanations, the support used, and what happens with another task. One response does not establish what you understand or predict a particular outcome.

An illustrative starting point

You want a short message to communicate a request clearly. You and Stephanie could compare two versions, notice what a reader can infer, and revise the wording. If spelling or reading a word interrupts the work, you can investigate that word and return to the message.

This is an example of a possible learning conversation, not a client story, assessment, or prescribed lesson. The actual starting point is agreed with you.

Questions you may bring

You can begin with a question.

Can I ask about support if I have never been diagnosed with dyslexia?

Yes. You can ask about reading, spelling, comprehension, or writing support without naming a diagnosis. Describe your goal or the task you want help with. A consultation explores educational fit; it does not diagnose dyslexia.

Is adult instruction the same as a children's program?

Your interests, experience, and goals guide the choice of work. Foundational language concepts can be taught explicitly using content appropriate to your age and interests. The starting point and supports are individualized rather than inferred from your age or a diagnostic label.

Does this provide a diagnosis or documentation for accommodations?

Lokahi Connect’s current services provide educational instruction and planning, not clinical diagnosis, speech-language pathology, or occupational therapy. If you need an evaluation or documentation for college or work, confirm the receiving institution’s requirements and the appropriate qualified professional. See the teen and adult resource guidance.

What should I share in the first inquiry?

Your adult contact details and permission to reply are enough. A general goal and time zone are helpful if you wish to include them. Keep diagnoses, evaluations, identifying learner information, and confidential workplace documents out of the inquiry. Arrange any sensitive-document exchange separately with Stephanie.
